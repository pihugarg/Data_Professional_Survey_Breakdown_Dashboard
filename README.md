# 📊 Data Professional Survey Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Data Professional Survey Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze survey responses from data professionals across different countries, job roles, salary ranges, programming language preferences, and workplace satisfaction metrics.

The dashboard transforms raw survey data into actionable insights, helping understand industry trends, compensation patterns, career challenges, and employee satisfaction within the data profession.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze the demographics of data professionals.
- Compare average salaries across different job roles.
- Identify the most preferred programming languages.
- Evaluate salary and work-life balance satisfaction.
- Understand the challenges of breaking into the data industry.
- Explore the geographical distribution of survey participants.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🌍 Country of Survey Takers
- Displays the geographical distribution of survey respondents.
- United States contributes the highest number of responses.
- India is the second-largest contributor.

### 💰 Average Salary by Job Title
- Compares average salaries across different data roles.
- Data Scientists report the highest average salaries.
- Students and job seekers report the lowest salaries.

### 👥 Survey Statistics
- Total Survey Takers: **630**
- Average Age: **29.87 Years**

### 🐍 Favorite Programming Language
- Python is the most preferred language among data professionals.
- R ranks second in popularity.
- Java, JavaScript, and C/C++ show lower adoption.

### 🚀 Difficulty to Break into Data
- Most respondents believe entering the data field is moderately challenging.
- Very few respondents consider it extremely easy or extremely difficult.

### 😊 Work-Life Balance Satisfaction
- Average Score: **5.74 / 10**
- Indicates moderate satisfaction among professionals.

### 💵 Salary Satisfaction
- Average Score: **4.27 / 10**
- Shows lower satisfaction with compensation compared to work-life balance.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Salary Analysis
- Data Scientists earn the highest average salary.
- Data Engineers and Data Architects follow closely.
- Specialized technical roles command higher compensation.

### Programming Language Trends
- Python dominates the data industry.
- R remains relevant for analytics and statistical work.
- Python is widely adopted across all major data roles.

### Career Insights
- Most professionals perceive the data field as moderately accessible.
- Practical experience and technical skills are crucial for career entry.

### Employee Satisfaction
- Work-life balance satisfaction exceeds salary satisfaction.
- Compensation remains a significant area of concern.
